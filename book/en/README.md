# Database Design Patterns

A catalog of database design patterns in the spirit of the "Gang of Four". The patterns describe recurring problems in schema design and working with data, and ways to solve them. The section on **anti-patterns** covers common mistakes.

Patterns are grouped by area of work. The [table of contents](SUMMARY.md) sets the order of the chapters and serves as the book's navigation.

The book was created by [Kirill Mokevnin](https://github.com/mokevnin).
